The Road That Made Me

Personal reflections · 2026

When I finished high school, I did not expect to go to university. My parents were getting older, and I worried that our family could not afford the cost of further education.

Then I applied to Passerelles Numériques Vietnam and received a two-year scholarship in Web Development and Software Testing. It opened a door I had not thought was within reach.

After graduating, I felt that IT might not be right for me. I tried working as a server at Pizza 4P’s in Da Nang. That experience helped me recognize that this kind of work was not the right fit, so I returned to technology and joined Green Global.

Later, I served in the Ministry of Public Security. After completing my service, I moved to Vinh, Nghe An for work, and eventually to Ho Chi Minh City.

My journey has not been a straight line. I have changed direction, started over in new environments, and gradually learned more about myself. I am still learning what it means to live with purpose.

I do not need a perfect journey. I want a meaningful one.
