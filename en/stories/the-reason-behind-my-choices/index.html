The Reason Behind My Choices

Personal reflections · 2026

My parents are farmers. I know how demanding their work can be, and how much they have done to give our family a better life. They did not have the opportunity to pursue a full education, but they hoped I would have a different future.

When I first left IT, I was trying to find work that suited me. Working in hospitality taught me something important: choosing a career is not only about what you enjoy in the moment. It is also about the life you want to build.

I returned to IT because it offered a path toward stronger financial stability, more opportunities to learn and the ability to care for my family. That was a practical choice, and an important one.

I hope that one day my parents can work less, rest more, travel to places they have never seen, enjoy good food, stay healthy and do the things they have always wanted to do. Above all, I want to see them smile more.

That hope is one of the reasons I continue to grow.

I want the people I love to have a life they can enjoy.
