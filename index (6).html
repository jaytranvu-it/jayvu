Becoming, Not Arriving

Personal reflections · 2026

I used to think that growing up meant knowing exactly what to do next. Experience has taught me that the path is rarely so clear.

I have tried different jobs, moved between cities and had to adapt to new environments. Each chapter gave me another chance to ask what matters, what fits, and what kind of person I want to become.

I value self-awareness, kindness, family and the freedom to explore. I enjoy time in nature, swimming, travelling and discovering different cultures. Those experiences remind me that a meaningful life is bigger than a career.

I am not finished becoming who I want to be. And I am comfortable with that.

Growth is a direction, not a destination.
